01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Built to support, not replace, auditor judgment.

The analyzer organizes three years of financial statement data, calculates eight ratios, and compares Target's historical movements with Walmart and Costco benchmarks. It is designed to surface questions for follow-up, not to determine whether a misstatement exists.

Professional opportunity: Audit associates and financial analysts often review ratios manually across multiple statements and peer filings, which can be time-consuming and inconsistent. This prototype brings those comparisons into a repeatable screening workflow, making it easier to prioritize areas for human investigation. In a team-reported comparison using prepared inputs, the review took 20 minutes manually and 7 minutes with the analyzer (13 minutes saved; 65% less elapsed time). The timing result is a small project exercise, not a generalized productivity claim.

03 / ACCOUNTING THEORY

Designed around accounting principles.

01

Agency Theory

Financial statement analysis helps users assess information reported by management when incentives and information access differ.

02

Decision Usefulness

Clear comparisons and documented assumptions aim to make financial information more relevant and interpretable.

03

Professional Skepticism

Risk indicators prompt follow-up questions and evidence gathering rather than automatic conclusions.

Theory-to-design connections: Agency Theory supports transparent comparisons; Decision Usefulness supports understandable ratio displays; Professional Skepticism supports N/A safeguards and auditor follow-up procedures.

The separate source-verification register contains 198 source-data records: 66 individually matched to selected original annual-report figures, 120 populated records not independently checked, and 12 blank or N/A records not independently resolved. No mismatch was identified among the 66 matched records. Efficiency comparison protocol

To evaluate time savings without inventing results, both methods must complete the same task: review Target's eight financial ratios for FY2023–FY2025, compare historical changes and the Walmart/Costco benchmarks, identify risk flags, and write a short follow-up summary.

MethodProcedureElapsed time
Manual baselineUsing the same prepared financial inputs, calculate and compare the ratios without the analyzer; record risk flags and a short conclusion.20 minutes (team-reported)
Analyzer-assistedUsing the same prepared inputs, use the workbook and dashboard to review ratios, risk flags, and follow-up conclusions.7 minutes (team-reported)

Measurement: Start a timer at the beginning of each method and stop after the written summary is complete. Record any calculation errors, missing values, and corrections. Repeat or counterbalance the order of methods if possible to reduce practice effects.

Time saved: Manual minutes minus analyzer minutes. Percent reduction: (Manual minutes minus analyzer minutes) ÷ manual minutes × 100. Report only actual observed times; do not treat estimated savings as measured evidence.

Team-reported efficiency result: Manual review took 20 minutes; analyzer-assisted review took 7 minutes. This represents 13 minutes saved and a 65% reduction in elapsed time for the comparison task. Times were reported by the project team and were not independently observed or verified.

Timing comparison reported complete. Independent verification of timing records and any difference in error rates remain outstanding; the two methods should use identical inputs and scope.

05 / GOVERNANCE & LIMITATIONS

Transparent inputs. Human accountability.

Data provenance and privacy

Use publicly available SEC annual reports. Record filing sources, fiscal-year dates, units, and any data adjustments. Do not use confidential employer or client information.

Human review and safeguards

Missing inputs display N/A rather than zero. Affected ratings are withheld. Risk scores are screening aids, not probabilities of misstatement, audit opinions, or substitutes for professional judgment.

Known limitations: Target gross interest expense was not independently verified, so Target interest coverage is withheld; peer interest coverage remains provisional. FY2022 statement presentation and 53-week FY2023 comparability require care. Peer business models differ; screening thresholds are judgment-based and not statistically validated.

AI assistance and human accountability

AI assistance was used to help draft and refine project documentation, website content, and verification workflows. AI-generated suggestions and explanations are not substitutes for original annual reports, independently checked calculations, or professional judgment. The student team is responsible for confirming all reported figures, assumptions, test results, and final interpretations. The prototype uses a rules-based ratio and scoring workflow; it does not independently determine whether financial statements contain misstatements.

When not to use this tool

Do not use the analyzer as an audit opinion, a stand-alone fraud detector, a substitute for substantive testing, or a basis for investment or credit decisions without independent corroboration. Missing, incomparable, or unverified inputs require manual review and may warrant withholding a risk label.
